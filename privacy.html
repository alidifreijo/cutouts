Privacy & house rules

cutouts is a personal, non-commercial project: a table of analog collages and a little studio to make your own. No ads, no tracking cookies, no accounts for visitors.

Who's behind it. Ali de Freijo, based in Spain, is responsible for the data described here. Contact: cutscenes.studio@gmail.com.

What gets kept

  • Things you send. If you glue a collage to the table or add scraps to the pile, the images you made and the optional title, story and name you typed are stored. Nothing else about you is attached to them. Photos are re-drawn in your browser before upload, which removes hidden data such as location.
  • Things in your browser. Your current sheet, your last signature and a few settings are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they survive a reload. They never leave your device; clear your site data to remove them.
  • Visit counts. Vercel Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

Why it's kept

  • What you send is kept because you chose to send it (your consent). You can take that back any time by writing to the address below, and it comes off the table.
  • Visit counts and backups are kept to keep the site running and safe (legitimate interest). Neither identifies you.

How long

  • Everything sent waits for a look first. If it's turned down, it's deleted right away, image included.
  • Published collages and scraps stay on the table until you ask for them to be taken down, or the project closes.
  • Encrypted backups of the database are kept for 90 days.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase stores the database and the images.
  • Vercel serves the website and the anonymous visit counts.
  • Google Fonts serves some of the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.
  • esm.sh and IMG.LY deliver the background-removal tool, only if you press "Remove background" in the studio. The image is processed in your browser and isn't sent to them.

Supabase and Vercel may handle data outside the EU, under the standard contractual clauses in their data processing terms.

Want something removed, corrected, or a copy of what you sent?

Write to cutscenes.studio@gmail.com with the collage's title or a screenshot, and it'll be taken down within a few days. You can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority (AEPD).

House rules

Gluing something to the table or adding scraps to the pile means you agree to these:

  • Only send what you can share. Your own collages, and scraps cut from printed matter you have to hand. No photos of people who didn't say yes.
  • Keep it kind. Nothing hateful, violent, sexual, or meant to hurt someone. No personal details such as phone numbers or addresses.
  • It's on the table for everyone. You let cutouts show what you send, and let others reuse your scraps in their own collages on this site. You keep any rights you have, and you can ask for it to come down whenever you like.
  • Everything gets a look first. cutouts can turn down, or take down later, anything at all, without having to explain.
  • No guarantees. It's a hobby project: it can change, break or close. If it closes, what you sent goes with it.

The images

The collages are made from found magazine imagery. They're shown here as personal, non-commercial artwork. If you own something that appears and want it gone, write to the address above.
